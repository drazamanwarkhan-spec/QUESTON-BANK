What does the app recognise?
  • Questions: Q1., Question 1, 1. — parts: (a), a), (i)
  • Marks: (5 marks), [5], Marks: 5 — CLOs/PLOs: CLO 1, (CLO 2 | PLO 1, 3)
  • Chapters/topics: Chapter 3: …, Topic: …, Week 4 – … (applied to the questions below)
  • Difficulty: Easy/Medium/Hard, Type: Numerical/Theory, Answer: …, tables, and data shared by several questions
