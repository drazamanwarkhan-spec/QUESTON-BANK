Connect Gemini to this app (one time, about 1 minute)

  1. Click Get my free key. Google AI Studio opens with the Google account already signed in to this browser.
  2. Click Create API key and copy the key.
  3. Paste it below and click Connect & send. From then on, one click sends your command to Gemini and the questions go straight onto the paper.

The key stays only on this computer (encrypted if you set a password) and is only ever sent to Google.

Or use the Gemini / Copilot website (copy & paste)

  1. Click Open Gemini or Open Copilot. Everything above, including your own questions, is copied as the prompt.
  2. In Gemini / Copilot press Ctrl+V. Add any command of your own if you like, then send.
  3. Copy the answer (the copy icon under the answer) and come back here. Click Paste answer from clipboard; the questions go straight to the paper.
